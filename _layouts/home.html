What we do

The Civic Data Alliance is Louisville's civic tech organization. We advocate for inclusion, diversity, equity, accessibility, openness, transparency and accountability in government, civic tech and open data.

We work to ethically liberate, improve, gather, define and report on public data, and to grow an inclusive civic tech community.

Get Involved

Volunteers of every skill level are welcome: coders, designers, researchers, organizers and curious neighbors.

COVID-19 Community Mapping Project

In early 2020 our volunteers mapped local COVID-19 cases across the Kentucky area. The map and data below are preserved as a historical record and are no longer updated. The project source is on GitHub at civicdata/ky-area-covid-19.

Data was sourced from:

  • CHFS.ky.gov
  • lexingtonky.gov
  • Cynthiana Democrat
